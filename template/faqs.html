FAQs

What is the cost of the MOFREY FX Academy course?

+
The MOFREY FX Academy course is priced at $400 USD. This fee covers all course materials and resources required for your two-week learning journey.

Do I need to pay the full course fee upfront?

+
Yes, all participants are required to clear the full balance before starting the class. This ensures a smooth registration process and allows you to fully focus on your learning experience.

How often are classes held at MOFREY FX Academy?

+
Classes are scheduled every month to accommodate a wide range of participants. Whether you're a beginner or looking to enhance your trading skills, there's a class for you each month.

Does MOFREY FX Academy provide accommodation or hostels?

+
No, MOFREY FX Academy does not provide accommodation or hostels. However, we can assist you in finding suitable accommodation options near our learning center.

Is there an age restriction for registering at MOFREY FX Academy?

+
No, there is no age restriction for registering at MOFREY FX Academy. We welcome participants of all ages who are eager to learn and excel in the field of forex trading.
